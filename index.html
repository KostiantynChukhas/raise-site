A new word every time
you raise your wrist.

Raise turns your Apple Watch face into a vocabulary trainer. 24 words a day, picked for your level by spaced repetition, in any pair of 10 languages: English, Ukrainian, Russian, Polish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Turkish.

How it works

Pick what you speak and what you're learning, take a two-minute level test, and install the face. Your watch shows the next word each time you look at it. Practise for two minutes a day and tomorrow's face is rebuilt from what you forgot.
